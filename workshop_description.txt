[h1]LFG Traditions[/h1]

Ten new tradition trees, seven council agendas, six ascension perks, fourteen premade empires, two origins, a civic, buildings, a policy and a decision for [b]Gigastructural Engineering[/b] games. Each tree fits specific empires and has a [b]hook[/b] that scales with your game state.

[h2]Tradition trees[/h2]

[h3]Megaforge[/h3]
[i]Megastructures over the normal economy.[/i] Needs Mega-Engineering or Supertensile Materials.
[list]
[*][b]Grand Works Priority:[/b] megastructures and infrastructure build faster; infrastructure costs 30% more.
[*][b]Monument Economy:[/b] +10 monthly supertensiles and a policy: per megastructure, favour megastructure or job output.
[*]Finisher: +1 Megaconstruction Logistics Hub (or +1 megastructure under construction).
[/list]

[h3]Shellworlders[/h3]
[i]Life on artificial worlds.[/i] Artificial-world origins, or after Orbital Habitats / Ring World.
[list]
[*]Artificial worlds: more efficient jobs, less housing use, more stability, less crime.
[*][b]Arc Neighbours:[/b] artificial worlds boost each other in a shared system.
[*][b]Downsides:[/b] slower growth and assembly; [b]Closed Ecology[/b] unsettles natural worlds.
[*][b]Arc Foundries:[/b] +5% megastructure build speed for every artificial world you own.
[*]Finisher: +1 max districts on artificial worlds.
[/list]

[h3]Xenoarchaeology[/h3]
[i]Scientists hunt anomalies and relics.[/i] Needs Ancient Relics.
[list]
[*]More anomalies; faster anomaly research and surveys.
[*][b]Anomaly Studies:[/b] +0.25% research speed per anomaly researched; each yields minor artifacts.
[*][b]Reverse Engineering:[/b] +5% research speed per relic you own.
[*][b]Commissioned Digs:[/b] buy a dig site, anomaly or astral rift with minor artifacts.
[*][b]Downside:[/b] scientists cost more.
[/list]

[h3]Stationcraft[/h3]
[i]Your wealth hangs in orbit.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*]Mining and research stations produce more for every colony, starbase, kilostructure and megastructure; jobs −35%.
[*]More, cheaper and tougher starbases; faster, cheaper megastructures with less upkeep.
[/list]

[h3]Lineage[/h3]
[i]Every trait is an advantage.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*][b]Strength Through Hardship:[/b] negative traits speed up pop growth.
[*][b]Honed Strengths:[/b] every positive trait makes jobs more efficient.
[*]Ship speed per species, research per genetic variant, diplomatic weight per planet type.
[*]Cheaper species modification; the finisher gives an extra trait point and pick.
[/list]

[h3]Penitence[/h3]
[i]Your penal colony runs the economy.[/i] Regular empires, needs Penal Colonies.
[list]
[*]Harder-working, better-guarded camps; less crime; the unemployed are sent to the colony.
[*][b]Idle Hands:[/b] colony jobs +1% per 100 unemployed, but they riot and die off.
[*][b]Prison Industry:[/b] +50% jobs from the colony's district specializations.
[*]Finisher: new Prison District specializations and a cell block expansion decision.
[*][b]Downsides:[/b] unhappier workers everywhere; Chain Gangs make the colony miserable.
[/list]

[h3]Abundance[/h3]
[i]Your food stockpile is political power.[/i] Only for empires whose main species eats food.
[list]
[*]More storage and much more productive farmers.
[*][b]Tithe Barns:[/b] full granaries bring stability and growth; empty ones, unrest.
[*]Up to +20% unity from stored food.
[*][b]Grain Reserves:[/b] huge food storage, but your people eat three times as much.
[*]Food surpluses buy influence.
[*]Finisher, [b]Bumper Crops:[/b] +1% organic pop growth per 1,000 monthly food.
[/list]

[h3]Broadcast[/h3]
[i]Entertainment for the whole galaxy.[/i] Non-gestalt empires.
[list]
[*]+1% trade per empire contacted (up to +20%).
[*][b]Holo-Museums:[/b] holomuseums grant their base bonuses again.
[*][b]Ratings War:[/b] less war exhaustion and more entertainer output while at war.
[*][b]Syndication Deals:[/b] entertainers earn trade per commercial pact.
[*]Finisher, [b]Star Power:[/b] +1% diplomatic weight per 1,000 entertainers (up to +30%).
[/list]

[h3]Tourism[/h3]
[i]Your resort world is the galaxy's jewel.[/i] Regular empires with Resort Colonies (Pleasure Worlds count).
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality Academies:[/b] +50% jobs from Accommodation Districts and resort specializations.
[*][b]Package Holidays:[/b] unity and happiness grow with your Resort Workers.
[*][b]Galactic Destination:[/b] Tourists produce trade and diplomatic weight.
[*][b]Paradise Unbound:[/b] more districts, plus the [b]Expand the Resort[/b] decision.
[*]Finisher: +10% job efficiency on the resort world.
[/list]

[h3]Tempest[/h3]
[i]Storms are an asset.[/i] Cosmic Storms DLC, after a first storm.
[list]
[*]Colonies and fleets grow stronger inside any storm; colonies build faster there.
[*]Research per storm weathered; unity per storm building.
[*]A new vortex-shaped tree.
[/list]

[h2]Council agendas[/h2]
Seven agendas (Galactic Paragons DLC), each lasting 10 years:
[list]
[*][b]The Great Project[/b] (Megaforge): much faster, cheaper megastructures (and more supertensiles); workers produce less.
[*][b]Grand Expedition[/b] (Xenoarchaeology): +50% anomaly, survey and dig speed; less research.
[*][b]Feast of Plenty[/b] (Granary): doubles Bumper Crops' growth.
[*][b]The Harvest Tithe[/b] (Granary): food into influence and unity, then more food.
[*][b]Gala Season[/b] (Broadcast): happiness and unity; trade suffers.
[*][b]Grand Tour Season[/b] (Tourism): happiness, trade and much harder-working resort workers.
[*][b]Storm Season[/b] (Tempest): research and stronger storm fields; less happiness.
[/list]

[h2]Origins[/h2]
[b]Swallowed Whole[/b] (non-machine): a leviathan spat your ring world out beyond the galaxy. Unseal it to find the way back; hunt the Matriarch.

[b]Nanite Forge[/b] (machines): a broken nanite harvester that Nanotech restores, plus nanite automation, unity and trade buildings.

After Nanotech: [b]Nanite Cognition Core[/b] (automates specialists), [b]Nanotensile Converter[/b] (supertensiles), [b]Nanite Pulse[/b] (overloads a harvester).

[h2]Civics[/h2]
[b]Applied Biosciences[/b] (Materialist, non-machine): Biologists trade some society for physics and engineering research.

[b]Galvanic civics:[/b] new galvanic armour and accelerator tiers, plus an X slot with A Celestial Armada.

[h2]Ascension perks[/h2]
[b]Mothership:[/b] one system-sized carrier per empire with escorts and a fleet aura; its forge boosts the navy.

[b]Nanite Armada:[/b] nanite Attack Moons and Planetcraft; faster, cheaper shipyards.

[b]Storm Sovereignty:[/b] summon nexus storms; Storm Chasers become storm-immune and turn them on enemies.

[b]Massed Assault:[/b] +10 ground combat width; every general boosts the armies they lead.

[b]Legendary Dragons[/b] (Here Be Dragons): drakes grow for decades and breathe splashing ice; one grows far beyond.

[b]Curated Progress[/b] (after Gigastructural Constructs): [b]Shelve[/b] edicts keep repeatables out of research.

[h2]Changes to vanilla content[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]: the uploading situation speeds up every year and uploads more pops.
[*]The [b]Accommodation District[/b], resort specializations, holomuseums and Archaeostudies Faculty gain tree bonuses.
[*]Repeatable techs check their Shelve edicts.
[*][b]Nanite harvesters[/b] double three more times on size 30+ planets.
[*][b]Virtuality[/b] is closed to individual machines with The Great Ring or Alderson Disk origins.
[/list]
Other mods changing these may conflict.
