[h1]LFG Traditions[/h1]

Eight new tradition trees, six council agendas, four ascension perks, an origin, a civic, buildings, a policy and a decision for [b]Gigastructural Engineering[/b] games. Each tree appears only for empires it fits and has a [b]hook[/b]: bonuses that scale with your game state.

[h2]Tradition trees[/h2]

[h3]Megaforge[/h3]
[i]Megastructures over the normal economy.[/i] Needs Mega-Engineering or Supertensile Materials.
[list]
[*]Works with supertensiles on or off.
[*][b]Grand Works Priority:[/b] megastructures and all infrastructure build faster, but infrastructure costs 30% more.
[*][b]Monument Economy:[/b] +10 monthly supertensiles and a policy: per megastructure, favour megastructure or job output.
[*]Finisher: +1 Megaconstruction Logistics Hub (or +1 megastructure under construction).
[/list]

[h3]Shellworlders[/h3]
[i]Life on artificial worlds.[/i] Artificial-world origins (including Gigastructural's), or after Orbital Habitats / Ring World.
[list]
[*]On your artificial worlds (habitats, rings, Alderson slices, Gigastructural worlds): more efficient jobs, less housing usage, more stability, less crime.
[*][b]Arc Neighbours:[/b] artificial worlds work better for each other one in their system.
[*][b]Downsides:[/b] slower growth and assembly; [b]Closed Ecology[/b] unsettles natural worlds.
[*][b]Arc Foundries:[/b] +5% megastructure build speed for every artificial world you own.
[*]Finisher: +1 max districts on artificial worlds.
[/list]

[h3]Xenoarchaeology[/h3]
[i]Scientists hunt anomalies and relics.[/i] Needs Ancient Relics.
[list]
[*]More anomalies, faster anomaly research and surveys.
[*][b]Anomaly Studies:[/b] +0.25% research speed per anomaly researched; each yields minor artifacts.
[*][b]Reverse Engineering:[/b] +5% research speed per relic you own.
[*][b]Commissioned Digs:[/b] spend 2,000 minor artifacts on a new dig site, anomaly or astral rift in your borders.
[*][b]Downside:[/b] scientists cost more.
[/list]

[h3]Lineage[/h3]
[i]Every trait is an advantage.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*][b]Strength Through Hardship:[/b] negative traits speed up pop growth.
[*][b]Honed Strengths:[/b] every positive trait makes jobs more efficient.
[*]Ship speed per species, society research per genetic variant of your main species, and diplomatic weight per planet type you live on.
[*]Cheaper species modification; the finisher gives an extra trait point and pick.
[/list]

[h3]Penitence[/h3]
[i]Your penal colony runs the economy.[/i] Regular empires, needs Penal Colonies.
[list]
[*]Harder-working, better-guarded camps; less crime; the unemployed are shipped to the colony.
[*][b]Idle Hands:[/b] colony jobs +1% per 100 unemployed, but they riot and die off.
[*][b]Prison Industry:[/b] +50% jobs from the colony's district specializations.
[*]Finisher: supertensile and bulk matter Prison District specializations; a decision to expand the cell blocks.
[*][b]Downsides:[/b] workers everywhere are less happy, and Chain Gangs make the colony miserable.
[/list]

[h3]Abundance[/h3]
[i]Your food stockpile is political power.[/i] Only for empires whose main species eats food.
[list]
[*]More storage and much more productive farmers.
[*][b]Tithe Barns:[/b] full granaries bring stability and growth; empty ones, unrest.
[*]Up to +20% unity from stored food.
[*][b]Grain Reserves:[/b] huge food storage, but your people eat three times as much.
[*]Food surpluses buy influence; farmers gain political power.
[*]Finisher, [b]Bumper Crops:[/b] +1% organic pop growth for every 1,000 food produced per month.
[/list]

[h3]Broadcast[/h3]
[i]Entertainment for the whole galaxy.[/i] Non-gestalt empires.
[list]
[*]+1% trade for every empire you have contact with (up to +20%).
[*][b]Holo-Museums:[/b] holomuseums grant their base bonuses again.
[*][b]Ratings War:[/b] less war exhaustion and more entertainer output while at war.
[*][b]Syndication Deals:[/b] entertainers earn trade per commercial pact.
[*]Finisher, [b]Star Power:[/b] +1% diplomatic weight per 1,000 entertainers (up to +30%).
[/list]

[h3]Tourism[/h3]
[i]Your resort world as the jewel of the galaxy.[/i] Regular empires, needs Resort Colonies. Works for Pleasure Worlds too.
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality Academies:[/b] +50% jobs from Accommodation Districts and resort specializations.
[*][b]Package Holidays:[/b] unity and happiness grow with your Resort Workers.
[*][b]Galactic Destination:[/b] Tourists produce trade and diplomatic weight.
[*][b]Paradise Unbound:[/b] more districts, plus the [b]Expand the Resort[/b] decision.
[*]Finisher: +10% job efficiency on the resort world.
[/list]

[h2]Council agendas[/h2]
Six new agendas (Galactic Paragons DLC), each lasting 10 years after launch:
[list]
[*][b]The Great Project[/b] (Megaforge): much faster, cheaper megastructures (and more supertensiles); workers produce less.
[*][b]Grand Expedition[/b] (Xenoarchaeology): +50% anomaly research, survey and excavation speed; less research.
[*][b]Feast of Plenty[/b] (Granary): doubles Bumper Crops' growth.
[*][b]The Harvest Tithe[/b] (Granary): food into influence and unity, then more food.
[*][b]Gala Season[/b] (Broadcast): happiness and unity; trade suffers.
[*][b]Grand Tour Season[/b] (Tourism): happiness, trade and much harder-working resort workers.
[/list]

[h2]Nanites[/h2]
[b]Nanite Forge[/b] (machines): a unique home system, a Starhold and a broken nanite harvester that doubles their nanites every 3 years (up to 12.8 each). Nanotech restores it, growing them far further. Unlocks a nanite-fuelled automation building.

After Nanotech: the [b]Nanite Cognition Core[/b] automates half of a planet's specialist jobs, and the [b]Nanotensile Converter[/b] turns energy and nanites into supertensiles.

[h2]Civic[/h2]
[b]Applied Biosciences[/b] (Materialist, non-machine): Biologists research -1 society, +0.4 physics, +0.4 engineering. Scientist councilor: more efficient Biologists.

[h2]Ascension perks[/h2]
[b]Mothership[/b] (needs Nanite Armada, A Celestial Armada and Nanotech finished): a [b]Progenitor Forge[/b] around an O star builds Motherships (100M nanites): stronger than a systemcraft, with nanite hangars and a growing swarmer escort.

[b]Nanite Armada[/b] (needs Nanotech): research and build a [b]Swarmforge[/b] around an A or B star or red giant, the only shipyard for designable nanite Attack Moons (500,000 nanites) and Planetcraft (1,000,000).

[b]Massed Assault:[/b] +10 ground combat width; every general boosts the armies they lead.

[b]Curated Progress[/b] (needs [b]Gigastructural Constructs[/b]): a [b]Shelve[/b] edict for every repeatable technology, stopping it from being offered as a research option (2,000 unity, 200 influence, plus monthly research).

[h2]Changes to vanilla content[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]: the uploading situation speeds up every year and uploads more pops.
[*]The [b]Accommodation District[/b], resort specializations, [b]holomuseums[/b] and [b]Archaeostudies Faculty[/b] gain extra blocks for our trees.
[*]Repeatable technologies (vanilla and Gigastructural) check their Shelve edicts.
[*][b]Nanite harvesters[/b] get three more doubling steps on planets of size 30+.
[*][b]Virtuality[/b] is closed to individual machines with Gigastructural's [b]The Great Ring[/b] or [b]Alderson Disk[/b] origin.
[/list]
These replace those vanilla and Gigastructural definitions; other mods that change them may conflict.
