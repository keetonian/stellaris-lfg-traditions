[h1]LFG Traditions[/h1]

Eight new tradition trees, seven council agendas, a new civic, a policy and a planet decision for [b]Gigastructural Engineering[/b] games. Every tree only appears for the empires it fits, and every tree has a [b]hook[/b]: bonuses that scale with or depend on your game state, so each one changes how you play rather than just adding percentages.

[h2]Tradition trees[/h2]

[h3]Megaforge Doctrine[/h3]
[i]Megastructures over the normal economy.[/i] Needs Mega-Engineering or Supertensile Materials.
[list]
[*]Works with Gigastructural's supertensiles on or off: with them, it boosts supertensile output, Supertensiles Technicians and Megaconstruction Directors, supertensile storage and upkeep; without them, the same slots give megastructure cost, upkeep, build speed and huge resource storage instead.
[*][b]Grand Works Priority:[/b] megastructures and all infrastructure build faster, but infrastructure costs 30% more.
[*][b]Monument Economy:[/b] +10 monthly supertensiles and unlocks the [b]Monument Economy[/b] policy. For every megastructure you own, choose [i]Monuments First[/i] (+1% megastructure output, -0.5% job output) or [i]People First[/i] (+0.5% job output, -1% megastructure output), or stay [i]Balanced[/i].
[*]Finisher: +1 Megaconstruction Logistics Hub (or +1 megastructure under construction at once without supertensiles).
[*]Costs: minerals, alloys and infrastructure cost.
[/list]

[h3]Attendant Robotics[/h3]
[i]Fewer, better robots that serve your people.[/i] Only for the [b]Pampered Masters[/b] civic.
[list]
[*]Robots work much harder while they're under 15% of your population, and get sloppy when mass-produced.
[*]Servant robots, happier organics, cheaper robot upkeep; robot assembly and roboticists are limited.
[*]Finisher: [b]doubles[/b] the civic's pampering bonuses.
[/list]

[h3]Overclocked Network[/h3]
[i]Complex drones over simple drones.[/i] Machine Intelligence only.
[list]
[*]Complex drones produce and work more; simple drones are throttled.
[*]Runs hot: an energy deficit cuts complex drone output by 25%; at war, complex drones push harder and ships fire faster.
[/list]

[h3]Shellworlders[/h3]
[i]Life on habitats, rings and frameworlds.[/i] Artificial-world origins (including Gigastructural's), or after Orbital Habitats / Ring World.
[list]
[*]Cheaper habitats and frameworlds, less housing usage, more stability; growth is limited.
[*]Finisher grows with every artificial world you own (2 / 5 / 10).
[/list]

[h3]Polymath Commons[/h3]
[i]Research from everyone.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*][b]Many Minds:[/b] +1% research speed per species in your empire (up to +30%).
[*][b]Rival Laboratories:[/b] +4% research speed per rival, but -5% with none.
[*][b]Reckless Experimentation:[/b] +15% research from jobs, and every year a colony may suffer a [b]Lab Accident[/b] (5%) or enjoy a [b]Lab Breakthrough[/b] (15%).
[*]Finisher: +15% anomaly discovery chance.
[/list]

[h3]Granary Kings[/h3]
[i]Your food stockpile is political power.[/i] Only for empires whose main species eats food.
[list]
[*]Full granaries mean stability and unity (up to +20% unity from stored food); empty ones mean unrest.
[*][b]Grain Reserves:[/b] +100,000 food storage, but your people eat twice as much.
[*]Food surpluses buy influence, and farmers gain political power over specialists.
[*]Finisher, [b]Bumper Crops:[/b] +1% organic pop growth for every 1,000 monthly food income, with no cap.
[/list]

[h3]Galactic Broadcast[/h3]
[i]Entertainment for the whole galaxy.[/i] Non-gestalt empires.
[list]
[*]+1% trade for every empire you have contact with (up to +20%).
[*][b]Holo-Museums:[/b] every Grand Archive holomuseum you own is twice as effective.
[*][b]Ratings War:[/b] war is prime time: less war exhaustion and more entertainer output while at war.
[*][b]Syndication Deals:[/b] entertainers earn more trade for every commercial pact you have.
[/list]

[h3]The Grand Tour[/h3]
[i]Your resort world as the jewel of the galaxy.[/i] Regular empires, needs Resort Colonies.
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality Academies:[/b] +50% Resort Worker jobs in every Accommodation District.
[*]Unity, trade and diplomatic weight grow with every 100 Resort Workers you employ, with no cap.
[*][b]Paradise Unbound:[/b] Accommodation Districts make room for more districts, and unlocks the [b]Expand the Resort[/b] decision: up to 5 expansions of +2 max districts each, for alloys, minerals, unity and influence. Together they can more than cancel the resort world's -25% district penalty.
[/list]

[h2]Council agendas[/h2]
Seven new agendas (Galactic Paragons DLC), each lasting 10 years after launch:
[list]
[*][b]The Great Project[/b] (Megaforge): much faster, cheaper megastructures (and more supertensiles); workers produce less.
[*][b]Overclock Protocol[/b] (Overclocked Network): complex drones +20%; simple drones and job upkeep pay for it.
[*][b]Eureka Season[/b] (Polymath): +20% research speed; -5 stability.
[*][b]Feast of Plenty[/b] (Granary): doubles Bumper Crops' growth.
[*][b]The Harvest Tithe[/b] (Granary): turns 3,000 food into influence and unity, then more food and stability.
[*][b]Gala Season[/b] (Galactic Broadcast): happiness and unity; trade suffers.
[*][b]Grand Tour Season[/b] (The Grand Tour): happiness, trade and much harder-working resort workers.
[/list]

[h2]Civic: Pampered Masters[/h2]
A reverse Rogue Servitor: an individualist, biological empire whose robots serve the organics.
[list]
[*]Organics are happier and use fewer amenities; robots and robot servants work harder.
[*][b]Strong downside:[/b] organic workforce -20%, robot assembly -40%, fewer and costlier roboticists, workers demand more consumer goods.
[*]Not available to gestalts, machine or mechanical species, or cybernetic/synthetic origins. [b]Blocks Cybernetic and Synthetic ascension.[/b]
[*]Unlocks the Attendant Robotics tradition tree.
[/list]

[h2]Changes to vanilla content[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Flesh is Weak[/b] and [b]Synthetic Evolution[/b] can't be taken with Pampered Masters.
[*][b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]: the origin's uploading situation gains +1 monthly progress for every year that has passed.
[*]The [b]Accommodation District[/b] and the three [b]holomuseums[/b] gain extra blocks for The Grand Tour and Galactic Broadcast.
[/list]
These replace the vanilla definitions of those two ascension perks, that situation, that district and those three buildings. Other mods that change the same definitions may conflict.
