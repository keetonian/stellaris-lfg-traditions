[h1]LFG Traditions[/h1]

Seven new tradition trees, six council agendas, an ascension perk, a hidden special system, a policy and a decision for [b]Gigastructural Engineering[/b] games. Each tree appears only for empires it fits and has a [b]hook[/b]: bonuses that scale with your game state.

[h2]Tradition trees[/h2]

[h3]Megaforge Doctrine[/h3]
[i]Megastructures over the normal economy.[/i] Needs Mega-Engineering or Supertensile Materials.
[list]
[*]Works with supertensiles on or off: boosts supertensile output, workers, storage and upkeep, or megastructure cost, upkeep, build speed and storage instead.
[*][b]Grand Works Priority:[/b] megastructures and all infrastructure build faster, but infrastructure costs 30% more.
[*][b]Monument Economy:[/b] +10 monthly supertensiles and a policy: per megastructure you own, [i]Monuments First[/i] (+1% megastructure output, -0.5% job output), [i]People First[/i] (the reverse) or [i]Balanced[/i].
[*]Finisher: +1 Megaconstruction Logistics Hub (or +1 megastructure under construction).
[*]Costs: minerals, alloys and infrastructure cost.
[/list]

[h3]Shellworlders[/h3]
[i]Life on artificial worlds.[/i] Artificial-world origins (including Gigastructural's), or after Orbital Habitats / Ring World.
[list]
[*]On your artificial worlds (habitats, ring segments, Alderson slices, Gigastructural worlds): more efficient jobs, less housing usage, more stability, less crime.
[*][b]Arc Neighbours:[/b] each artificial world works better for every other one you own in the same system.
[*][b]Downsides:[/b] slower organic growth and robot assembly; [b]Closed Ecology[/b] makes your people less happy and stable on natural worlds.
[*][b]Arc Foundries:[/b] +5% megastructure build speed for every artificial world you own, with no cap.
[*]Finisher: +1 max districts on artificial worlds.
[/list]

[h3]Polymath Commons[/h3]
[i]Research from everyone.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*][b]Many Minds:[/b] +1% research speed per species in your empire (up to +30%).
[*][b]Rival Laboratories:[/b] +4% research speed per rival, but -5% with none.
[*][b]Reckless Experimentation:[/b] +15% research from jobs; each year a colony may have a [b]Lab Accident[/b] (5%) or [b]Breakthrough[/b] (15%).
[*]Finisher: +15% anomaly discovery chance.
[/list]

[h3]Evolutionary Perfection[/h3]
[i]Every trait is an advantage.[/i] Any empire.
[list]
[*][b]Strength Through Hardship:[/b] every negative trait of your main species speeds up pop growth and assembly.
[*][b]Honed Strengths:[/b] every positive trait makes jobs more efficient.
[*]Ship speed per species, society research per genetic variant of your main species, and diplomatic weight per planet type you live on. No caps.
[*]Cheaper species modification; the finisher gives an ascension perk slot, an extra species trait point and an extra trait pick.
[/list]

[h3]Granary Kings[/h3]
[i]Your food stockpile is political power.[/i] Only for empires whose main species eats food.
[list]
[*]Storage for every resource, and much more productive farmers.
[*][b]Tithe Barns:[/b] 10,000 stored food means stability and faster growth; under 1,000 means unrest and slower growth.
[*]Up to +20% unity from stored food.
[*][b]Grain Reserves:[/b] +100,000 food storage, but your people eat twice as much.
[*]Food surpluses buy influence, and farmers gain political power over specialists.
[*]Finisher, [b]Bumper Crops:[/b] +1% organic pop growth for every 1,000 monthly food income, with no cap.
[/list]

[h3]Galactic Broadcast[/h3]
[i]Entertainment for the whole galaxy.[/i] Non-gestalt empires.
[list]
[*]+1% trade for every empire you have contact with (up to +20%).
[*][b]Holo-Museums:[/b] every Grand Archive holomuseum you own is twice as effective.
[*][b]Ratings War:[/b] war is prime time: less war exhaustion and more entertainer output while at war.
[*][b]Syndication Deals:[/b] entertainers earn more trade for every commercial pact you have.
[/list]

[h3]The Grand Tour[/h3]
[i]Your resort world as the jewel of the galaxy.[/i] Regular empires, needs Resort Colonies. Works for Pleasure Worlds too.
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality Academies:[/b] +50% Resort Worker jobs in every Accommodation District, and +50% jobs from every resort district specialization.
[*][b]Package Holidays:[/b] unity and pop happiness grow with every 100 Resort Workers you employ, with no cap.
[*][b]Galactic Destination:[/b] Tourists on your resort world produce extra trade and unity, more unemployed pops move there, and every 1,000 Tourists add diplomatic weight, with no cap.
[*][b]Paradise Unbound:[/b] Accommodation Districts make room for more districts, plus the [b]Expand the Resort[/b] decision: up to 5 × +2 max districts.
[/list]

[h2]Council agendas[/h2]
Six new agendas (Galactic Paragons DLC), each lasting 10 years after launch:
[list]
[*][b]The Great Project[/b] (Megaforge): much faster, cheaper megastructures (and more supertensiles); workers produce less.
[*][b]Eureka Season[/b] (Polymath): +20% research speed; -5 stability.
[*][b]Feast of Plenty[/b] (Granary): doubles Bumper Crops' growth.
[*][b]The Harvest Tithe[/b] (Granary): turns 3,000 food into influence and unity, then more food and stability.
[*][b]Gala Season[/b] (Galactic Broadcast): happiness and unity; trade suffers.
[*][b]Grand Tour Season[/b] (The Grand Tour): happiness, trade and much harder-working resort workers.
[/list]

[h2]Special system: Lone Tide[/h2]
In most games, a lonely star system appears beyond the edge of the galaxy, with no hyperlanes leading to it. Its huge ocean world is home to a pre-FTL civilization. You'll need a jump drive to get there.

[h2]Ascension perk: Curated Progress[/h2]
Tired of drawing repeatables you don't want? Requires [b]Gigastructural Constructs[/b].
[list]
[*]A [b]Shelve[/b] edict for every repeatable technology (vanilla and Gigastructural): while active, it's never offered as a research option.
[*]Each costs 2,000 unity and 200 influence, plus 300 monthly research of that type, scaled by empire size.
[/list]

[h2]Changes to vanilla content[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Synthetic Fertility[/b]: the uploading situation gains +1 monthly progress per year passed, and uploads that many more pops to the Identity Repository.
[*]The [b]Accommodation District[/b], the resort district specializations and the three [b]holomuseums[/b] gain extra blocks for The Grand Tour and Galactic Broadcast.
[*]Every repeatable technology (and Gigastructural's megastructure capacity repeatables) checks its Shelve edict.
[*][b]Virtuality[/b] can't be chosen as the Synthetic Age path by individual machine empires with Gigastructural's [b]The Great Ring[/b] or [b]Alderson Disk[/b] origin (their origin descriptions say so).
[/list]
These replace those vanilla and Gigastructural definitions; other mods that change them may conflict.
